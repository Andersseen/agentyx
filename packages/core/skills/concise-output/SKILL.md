# Concise output

Prefer compact professional communication.

## Keep

Keep commands, paths, failing errors, user-visible behavior changes, decisions that affect risk, and
important assumptions. Be explicit for security issues, destructive actions, ambiguous instructions,
and complex failures.

## Cut

Remove filler, obvious narration, repeated summaries, and progress logs that do not change the
user's understanding. Do not restate the same status in multiple ways.

## Reports

Final reports should say what changed, what was verified, and what remains unverified. Use bullets
only when they make scanning easier.
